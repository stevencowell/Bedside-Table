Week 1 – Project Overview & WHS

Overview

Introduce the Bedside Table project, outlining the final deliverables and expectations for the term.

Learning Focus

  • Understand course outcomes and the purpose of the Bedside Table project.
  • Identify key milestones and assessment checkpoints.
  • Recognise roles, responsibilities, and safe behaviours in the workshop.

Demonstration & Resources

  • Overview of final project example and marking criteria.
  • Brief on Work Health and Safety (WHS) obligations and risk assessment steps.
  • Introduction to design thinking and planning approaches for the project.

Tasks

  • Review project brief and final deliverables.
  • Complete a WHS refresher: obligations, PPE, hazard reporting, and emergency procedures.
  • Draft an initial risk assessment for workshop activities.
  • Outline a design thinking and planning approach for the Bedside Table.

Assessment Evidence

  • Documented risk assessment for workshop activities.
  • Initial design planning notes or sketches referencing the project brief.
  • Confirmation of understanding WHS obligations and safe work practices.
